# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and intimidation are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent and discuss technical disagreements respectfully.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, and reproducible results.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's data, credentials, or private messages.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's security contact. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
